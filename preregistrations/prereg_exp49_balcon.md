## 1. The hypothesis and its origin (disclosed)
Exp48 (17c02ab) returned INDETERMINADO on the ρ axis, and its labeled post-hoc
descriptive suggested why: ρ does not determine λ near the edge, and re-binning the same
128 networks by measured λ showed R-dim rising monotonically to 7.88 in the stratum
(−0.010, −0.005] and DROPPING to 6.80 at the edge itself (|λ| ≤ 0.005). Declared
hypothesis: **the maximum of R-dim lies strictly inside stability ("the balcony"), not
at the edge ("the rim")** — at exact criticality, trial decoherence already collects its
toll, while slight contraction preserves coherence with near-critical memory. Secondary
(from the sealed exp46 result that coherent-response DURATION peaks at the edge): if
R-dim peaks inside stability while T_c peaks at the edge, maximum coherent TIME and
maximum complexity DISSOCIATE — coherence quality matters beyond duration.
